☀️ Solar PV System

Solar photovoltaic contribution in the standalone microgrid

Working Principle


Solar photovoltaic panels convert incident solar radiation into electrical energy using photovoltaic cells. The generated power varies according to solar availability.

Input

Solar irradiance and environmental conditions.

Conversion

Photovoltaic conversion produces electrical power.

Output

Electrical power supplied to the standalone microgrid.

Advantage

Clean renewable generation with no fuel requirement.
