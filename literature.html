Literature Survey

Research background related to hybrid renewable energy microgrids

Research Background


Previous research on hybrid renewable energy systems has focused on integrating multiple renewable sources, energy storage and backup generation to improve the reliability and sustainability of standalone power systems.


Optimization-based approaches are used to determine the suitable participation of different sources while satisfying load demand, generation limits and battery operating constraints.

Research Area 01

Hybrid renewable energy source integration for standalone microgrids.

Research Area 02

Optimal scheduling and power management of renewable sources and storage.

Research Area 03

Battery energy storage for balancing variable renewable generation.

Research Area 04

Optimization of operating cost, renewable utilization and system reliability.

Research Gap Considered


The proposed project focuses on coordinated participation of Solar PV, Wind, Hydrokinetic Energy, Battery Storage and Diesel Generator in a standalone microgrid using an optimization-based approach.
