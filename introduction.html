Introduction

Optimal Participation of Hybrid Renewable Energy Sources in Standalone Microgrid

Background


The increasing demand for sustainable and reliable electricity has encouraged the development of hybrid renewable energy based standalone microgrid systems.


A standalone microgrid is an independent power system that can supply electrical loads without continuous dependence on the main utility grid.


Hybrid renewable energy systems combine multiple renewable energy sources with energy storage and backup generation. This combination can improve energy availability and system reliability.


The proposed project focuses on the optimal participation of Solar PV, Wind Energy and Hydrokinetic Energy together with Battery Storage and Diesel Generator.

☀️ Renewable Sources

Solar PV, wind and hydrokinetic sources provide renewable power.

🔋 Energy Storage

Battery storage helps balance generation and demand.

🛢️ Backup Generation

Diesel generation can support the system when renewable generation is insufficient.

🧠 Optimization

Optimization determines suitable participation of different sources while satisfying system requirements.
