🌬️ Wind Energy

Wind turbine participation in the hybrid microgrid

Working Principle


A wind turbine converts the kinetic energy of moving air into mechanical rotation and subsequently into electrical energy. The generated power depends on the available wind condition.

Wind Resource

Wind speed determines the available generation.

Mechanical Conversion

Rotor blades capture kinetic energy from wind.

Electrical Generation

The generator converts mechanical energy into electricity.

Microgrid Contribution

Wind generation reduces dependence on conventional backup.
