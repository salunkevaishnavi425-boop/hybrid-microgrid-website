⚙️ Methodology

Procedure followed for optimal operation

01. Define Load

Determine the electrical load demand for the operating period.

02. Define Renewable Sources

Model Solar PV, Wind and Hydrokinetic generation profiles.

03. Add Storage

Include battery power and state-of-charge constraints.

04. Add Backup

Include diesel generator as a controllable backup source.

05. Formulate Optimization

Define objective function and operating constraints.

06. Solve

Use numerical optimization to determine source participation.

07. Analyze Results

Evaluate generation, battery SOC, cost and renewable contribution.

08. Compare

Compare source participation and overall system performance.
