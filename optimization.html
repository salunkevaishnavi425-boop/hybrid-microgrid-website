🧠 Optimization

Optimal participation of hybrid energy sources

Optimization Concept


The optimization problem determines the participation of different energy sources while satisfying the required power balance and operating constraints.

Minimize: Objective Function

Subject to:
Power Balance Constraints
Source Power Limits
Battery Power Limits
Battery SOC Constraints

Decision Variables

P_HKT, P_PV, P_WT, P_DG and P_B represent source participation variables.

Objective

Determine a suitable operating point considering system requirements.

Constraints

Generation and storage variables must remain within their specified limits.

Solver

The project model uses numerical constrained optimization to determine the solution.
