🌊 Hydrokinetic Energy

Hydrokinetic generation in the proposed microgrid

Working Principle


Hydrokinetic systems extract energy from the kinetic movement of flowing water. The water flow drives a turbine, which is connected to an electrical generator.

Water Flow

Flowing water provides the available kinetic energy.

Turbine

The turbine converts water movement into mechanical rotation.

Generator

Mechanical energy is converted into electrical power.

Renewable Support

Hydrokinetic generation provides an additional renewable source.
