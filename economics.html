Operating cost considerations

Economic Objective


An important purpose of optimal source participation is to avoid unnecessary operation of expensive conventional generation and improve the overall operating performance.

Fuel Cost

Diesel operation can involve fuel-related operating cost.

Renewable Generation

Renewable sources do not require conventional fuel during operation.

Battery Operation

Battery scheduling influences how surplus and deficit energy are managed.

Optimization

Optimization helps determine a suitable operating schedule.

Total Operating Cost = Renewable Operation Cost + Battery Operation Cost + Backup Generation Cost
