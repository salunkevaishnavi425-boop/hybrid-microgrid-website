🔋 Battery Energy Storage

Energy storage and power balancing

Role of Battery


The battery stores surplus electrical energy and supplies power when renewable generation is not sufficient to meet the load demand.

Charging

Battery absorbs surplus available energy.

Discharging

Battery supplies energy during generation deficit.

SOC

State of Charge indicates the stored energy level.

Power Limit

Battery charging and discharging are restricted by operating limits.

P_B = Battery Power

SOC = State of Charge
