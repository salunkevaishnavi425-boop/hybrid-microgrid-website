System Architecture

Proposed standalone hybrid renewable energy microgrid

☀️

Solar PV

Converts solar energy into electrical power for the microgrid.

🌬️

Wind Turbine

Provides electrical generation according to wind availability.

🌊

Hydrokinetic Source

Provides additional renewable generation to the standalone system.

🔋

Battery Storage

Stores surplus energy and supplies power during deficit conditions.

🛢️

Diesel Generator

Provides backup generation when renewable sources and storage are insufficient.

🏠

Electrical Load

Represents the demand that must be continuously supplied.

Power Balance

P_HKT + P_PV + P_WT + P_DG + P_B = P_Load

The generated power from the available sources and battery participation must satisfy the required load demand.
