🛢️ Diesel Generator

Backup generation for reliable standalone operation

Role of Diesel Generator


The diesel generator acts as a backup source when the available renewable generation and battery contribution cannot completely satisfy the load demand.

Backup Source

Supports the microgrid during power deficit.

Reliability

Helps maintain supply during insufficient renewable availability.

Controlled Operation

Optimization can limit unnecessary diesel generation.

Fuel Consumption

Reduced operation can help lower fuel-related operating cost.
